Our Story

Baked fresh every morning

Paul & Jack is a family-owned bakery led by pastry chef Jacques and his wife Alina, bringing European-inspired craft desserts from their home country to Wake Forest and Downtown Raleigh.

Every visit is built around balance — desserts that are beautiful without being overly sweet, croissants with shatteringly crisp layers, and espresso drinks pulled with care. Dine in, grab takeout, or enjoy our outdoor seating.
